A few things worth knowing

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Your confidence starts with understanding how your conversations work.

What does the Verified badge mean?

Joschat checks that the stored encrypted message matches its recorded hash and ledger links. Verified means those integrity checks passed. It does not verify someone’s identity or whether a message is true.

Can I send messages without a connection?

In a chat you have already opened and unlocked, text messages can wait in an encrypted outbox and retry when you reconnect. Opening chat history needs a connection, and attachments must upload online first. If your session expires, sign in again to resume delivery.

How do encrypted group chats work?

Groups use a shared passphrase that members enter to unlock the conversation. Agree on it privately outside Joschat. Direct chats can use secure keys when both people have them set up.

Can I use Joschat on my phone?

Yes. Open Joschat in your mobile browser. Where supported, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option to keep it one tap away. You can also use it in your desktop browser.

Is my phone number visible to other people?

No. Your phone number is collected during registration and kept private. People find you by your username. Signing in still uses your email and password.
